# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **HireSync** project a harassment-free and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other team members.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback in code reviews.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall project team and candidate/recruiter user experience.
- Documenting decisions in GitHub Discussions before implementing major changes.

### Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of team members.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Intentionally introducing backdoors, malicious code, or security vulnerabilities into the codebase.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement & Responsibilities

Project leaders and repository administrators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code edits, issues, and pull requests that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- Git repositories and pull request comments
- GitHub Issues and Discussions
- Team chat channels and live sessions
- Live WebRTC / pair-programming sessions

---

## Conflict Resolution & Communication Guidelines

1. **Constructive Code Reviews**: Review code with empathy. Point out issues objectively, offer concrete suggestions, and acknowledge good solutions.
2. **Open Discussion**: Discuss architectural decisions transparently using GitHub Discussions before implementation.
3. **Escalation**: If a conflict arises that cannot be resolved amicably, bring it to the project leads for mediation.

---

## Enforcement Contact

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at: `conduct@hiresync.internal`

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
